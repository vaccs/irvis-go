About IRVIS

IRVis (Integer Representation Visualization) Author: Steve Carr This work has been supported by the National Science Foundation under grants DUE-1245310, DGE-1522883, DGE-1523017, IIS-1456763, and IIS-1455886. The original desktop application was written by James Walker.

Features:
• Interactive data visualization
• Issue reporting and tracking
• User management (Admin only)
• Secure authentication system
